# Functional-Renormalisation-of-incompressible-fully-developed-turbulence.
Here, we attempt a non perturbative renormalisation of incompressible Navier Stokes equations with a symmetry preserving derivative expansion truncation. The frequency dependence of such an ansatz for the effective vertex functional comprises the novelty in this work.
Objectives : 1. Energy and two point structure function scaling in isotropic fully developed turbulence realised from a non trivial frequency dependency of the derivative expansion scheme.
            2. Ward identities ensuing from the extended symmetries, need not satisfy due to the truncation. Investigate if the non trivial frequency dependence of the derivative scheme enhance the order to which the solution satisfies the ward identities.

The pdf document titled 'frg_ns_equations.pdf' includes the key derived Wetterich equation for the flow of frequency and momentum dependent vertex functions. The truncation scheme retains non trivial frequency dependence of the vertex functions and hence their RG flow.
'FRG_Solver_explcit_gpu.ipynb' is a numerical solver of these equations implemented in GPU.
